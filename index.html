Filing structure · proposal

The proposal

File by the LLC that owns it

The issue

Nothing on a document says whether a building is commercial or residential. That classification lives in people's heads, so filing by topic only works for the people who already know.

The solution

The entity is printed right on the paperwork. The insurance names the insured, the note names the borrower, the invoice names the bill-to. So make ownership the organizing question, and let everything below it be forgiving.

Two roots

The structure

Inside any LLC
  • Formation
  • Investors
  • Financials tax, AP, loans, banking
  • Insurance
  • Misc
  • Properties/ one folder per building
Inside any building
  • Purchase
  • Leases & Tenants
  • Construction
  • Operations
  • Photos & Media
  • Sale
  • Misc

Six folders in every LLC, the same six every time. Seven in every building. Nothing below them is enforced, so split Financials into tax years or AP however you like.

Inside Shared
Vendors Templates Contacts Playbooks Training Master Insurance Misc

Two rules

The vendor's identity is shared. The transaction is not.

A W9 and ACH details live once, in Shared. The invoice lives in the LLC whose account pays it.

In the folder is good enough.

Below these folders nothing is enforced. Add subfolders freely. Misc accepts anything, so nobody is ever stuck deciding.
